# Retro design language

- Page titles: a coloured banner row (`banner()`), white text on blue, red for the haunted pages, magenta for games and ghosts.
- Big headings use double height (`dbl()`), yellow by default.
- Yellow is for page numbers and headings, white for body text, cyan for hints and metadata, green for good news, red for warnings.
- Lines are short. Wrap at 38 columns, indent continuation lines.
- Use mosaics for weather icons, charts and frames. Keep icons to 6 by 3 cells.
- The footer always offers four sensible fastext choices. Labels are 9 characters or fewer, uppercase.
- Humour is dry. A wrong page number should feel like a missing broadcast, not an error message.
- No emoji, no lowercase shouting, no exclamation marks in body text.
